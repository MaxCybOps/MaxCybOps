## What I'm Building

### Syfx AI — Verifiable Agentic Finance Operating System
> *Trade without Trust. Proven by Math.*

Syfx AI lets anyone describe a trading strategy in plain English and deploys it as a fully autonomous AI agent that executes trades 24/7 across crypto and traditional Forex markets — with a **Zero-Knowledge cryptographic proof** attached to every single decision before execution.

**The core problem it solves:** Every AI trading platform today is a black box. You give them your money and hope the algorithm did what it said. Syfx AI fixes this permanently. The Triple Proof Chain — Signal Proof, Execution Proof, and a ZK-SNARK Receipt stored on the 0G blockchain — means every trade is mathematically verifiable by anyone, forever.

- Non-custodial smart contract vaults. Syfx AI never holds your funds.
- Multi-source Oracle monitoring X, Reddit, Polymarket, Telegram, news, and on-chain ICT/SMC patterns simultaneously.
- Advisory Mode: the AI researches and verifies, you approve before anything executes.
- Strategy Marketplace where every performance record is on-chain and impossible to fake.
- Supports Ethereum, Solana, Base, and 0G Network at launch.
